# Driftbox privacy note

- Driftbox has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It sends nothing to the developer.
- It only connects to Mail.tm (api.mail.tm and mercure.mail.tm) to create addresses, read mail and get live updates.
- Your addresses and passwords are stored encrypted on your phone only. Android backup is turned off.
- Emails are stored on Mail.tm's servers, not by Driftbox. Mail.tm's own terms and privacy policy apply to them.
- Disposable mail is not private: anyone who knows an address and its password can read it. Do not use it for anything sensitive.
- Notifications (optional) show the sender and subject only when your lock screen allows sensitive content.
- Deleting an address in Driftbox deletes it, and its emails, from Mail.tm.
